# The Meridian: layout and operation

Decided with the design lead; the drawing is `docs/ship-general-arrangement.html` (side elevation, deck plan, bow view, and why the drive sits in the keel).

## How she flies

- **Built in orbit, never makes landfall.** Nothing aerodynamic; exposed truss, add-on tanks and modules.
- **Thrust gravity, from a keel drive.** The main drive fires down out of the keel, through the centre of mass, so under thrust the floor faces the drive and the ship climbs roof first. That lets the whole crew deck run bow to stern on **one level**: no stairs or ladders anywhere.
- **Outside:** an armoured dorsal shield (water and ice tanks) faces the direction of travel; radiator wings sit out from the keel in its shadow; a shadow shield separates the reactor from the crew; a docking collar and airlock to port; a probe bay forward under the hull; thruster quads at bow and stern; a camera and sensor cluster on the bow face; on the back, the stub of the long-range array lost in the shear. About 57 m long, a 14 m hull, a 24 m shield, 13 m tall.
- **Stations.** Before a manoeuvre (burns, the turnover, the shear, anomaly approaches) the Proctor calls stations: on the bridge, the Captain, Pilot and Sensors (and Science for an anomaly); in situ, the Engineer at the reactor, Life Support at hydroponics, the Medic in the med bay (or the suit bay for EVA). Each in-situ room has its own harnessed seat; the bridge has a repeater panel for each.
- **The bridge** is about two-thirds modern control room, one-third starship: a single viewscreen across the front (the forward view from the bow cameras; at their speed the stars crowd ahead into a ring of shifted colour), Helm (Kit, with manual thruster controls) in the middle of a U with Sensors and Survey either side, the Captain standing at a plot table behind them (a fold-down harnessed seat for burns), repeater panels and jump seats along the back. Directly above the plot table, an **armoured viewport** looks straight up through the shield along the course: the ship's one real window, shuttered when not needed.

## The deck

One corridor: the **spine**, bow to stern. Every room opens onto it.

- **Bow:** the bridge. The **Ready Room** opens only onto the bridge (desk, chair, a small couch).
- **Forward:** starboard, three crew cabins (from the bridge: Kit, Rowan, Sefa); port, the Ready Room and the Captain's cabin (its own head and shower).
- **Amidships:** starboard, the **galley and mess**, running from Sefa's cabin to Morgan's, open wide onto the spine. Port, the **suit bay**, from the Captain's cabin aft, with the airlock and docking collar at its forward end, and the **med bay** next to it.
- **Aft:** starboard, three more cabins (Morgan, Noor, Sam) and the **communal showers**, warmed by the reactor's heat beyond the shadow shield. Port, **hydroponics and life support**, and the **workshop** (repairs, fabrication, spares).
- **Stern:** **reactor and propulsion**, the core filling the middle of the room with a walkway and guard rail round it.
- Each crew cabin has its own head; everyone but the Captain shares the communal showers (co-ed, matter-of-fact). The showers are where the marks of a correction are seen by the rest, and where word of the Captain's methods travels ahead of the formal introductions.
- The design doc's maintenance crawlspace is a crawl-through service duct beside the spine, at deck level.

## Where corrections happen

| Room | Across the knee | Standing | Over the table | Aftercare |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Suit bay | the suit bench | the grab rail | | the suit bench |
| Galley and mess | the end of a crew bench | hands on the island | the end of the crew table | the soft seating at the edge |
| Crew quarters (private: no witnesses) | the edge of the bunk | hands flat on the wall | | the edge of the bunk |
| Reactor | a parts crate | the guard rail round the core | | taken elsewhere (hydroponics, or their quarters) |
| Med bay | the edge of the recovery cot | hands on the exam table | over the exam table, set to their hip height | the cot |
| Hydroponics | the garden bench | the edge of the potting bench | | the garden bench |
| Ready Room | the couch | hands on the desk | over the desk | the couch |

- **The bridge is never used for corrections.** When the crew are gathered there it's mission-critical; once that phase has passed, the Captain takes anyone who needs one into the Ready Room.
- **Aftercare** happens in the room by default; the Captain can take them to the garden bench in hydroponics, or to their own quarters.
- **Crew quarters are private.** A correction in a cabin happens in the subject's own, with nobody watching: witnesses, and anyone waiting their turn, stay outside.
- Rooms are built one at a time (the galley and mess first, then the crew quarters), each its own scene for now, sized and doored to the plan so they can join into the walkable deck for patrols.
