Why the keel where the drive goes decides the decks

What it resembles. In profile it sits between Nostromo's industrial tug and Serenity's single working deck, with Hermes from The Martian behind the radiators and the long-haul mission feel. The armoured back and the drive firing out of the keel are the Meridian's own.
