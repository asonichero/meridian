# Game Design Document: Project Meridian (Working Title)

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

* **Genre:** Deep-Space Sci-Fi / Psychological Action-Adventure & Management Simulation.
* **Core Philosophy:** Authority as emotional gravity. In extreme isolation, strict physical discipline and embodied accountability serve as protective care and psychological stabilization rather than mere punishment.
* **Setting:** The *Ares/Nostromo*-class long-range exploration vessel, built for heavy industrial utility, high temporal dilation, and extreme operational lifespans.
* **Target Experience:** High-density spatial exploration, systemic shipboard management, in-situ/escorted discipline, and deep character-driven narrative webs with an evolving switch dynamic between the Player and the Ship’s AI.

---

## 2. World & High-Stakes Premise

### 2.1 The Outbound Parallax Survey
The ship is deployed on the furthest exploratory mission from Earth ever attempted. During the transit, an unexpected spatial shear permanently destroys the long-range FTL communication array and accelerates relativistic time dilation.

### 2.2 The Relativistic Crisis
Months aboard ship correspond to years or decades accumulating on Earth. The hard communication blackout removes the psychological anchor of "home". Standard corporate/scientific behavioral protocols collapse because remote HR counseling and Earth-bound metrics no longer have meaning, causing crew members to fracture under temporal dissociation.

### 2.3 The Archaic Pivot
Facing protocol decay and catastrophic mechanical failure, the Captain (Player) turns to historical, high-tactile methods of accountability. Guided by the ship's archival AI, the Captain institutes embodied discipline to ground the crew in present reality and preserve life-support integrity.

## 3. Core Gameplay Loop

```
                         [ MORNING PHASE ]
           Shipwide Telemetry Review & AI Briefing
                               │
                               ▼
                         [ DECK PATROL ]
          Walk Compartments · Inspect Systems & Gear
                               │
            ┌──────────────────┴──────────────────┐
     [ MINOR INFRACTION ]                 [ SEVERE / TRAP ]
            │                                     │
   In-Situ Public Reset                 Escort to Private Node
  (Calisthenics, Posture)             (Detailed Session & Aftercare)
            │                                     │
            └──────────────────┬──────────────────┘
                               │
                               ▼
                       [ EVENING PHASE ]
            Command Check-In & AI Evaluation (Switch)
```

1. **Morning Phase (Diagnostics):** Review ship metrics, systemic decay, and individual crew stability meters (**Stress**, **Wilfulness**, **Resentment**, **Compliance**).
2. **Shift Phase (Patrol & Inspection):** Explore ship compartments, check telemetry, identify hidden maintenance errors, and enforce shift protocols.
3. **Discipline Loop:**
   * **In-Situ (Public):** Handle minor slacking, bad posture, or minor cheek right where the crewmate stands.
   * **Escorted (Private):** Lead defiant, burning-out, or traumatized crewmates to the nearest private node (Airlock, Maintenance Crawlspace, Hydroponics) for a structured correction session.
4. **Restoration & Aftercare:** Complete every session with explicit restitution (rest details, assigned log resets, warm reassurances) to clear Resentment while maintaining high Compliance.
5. **Night Phase (Command Check-In):** Report to the AI in the ready room. If the player's performance slips, the AI temporarily assumes command to hold the player accountable.

## 4. Ship Architecture & Discipline Nodes

The vessel features a compact, industrial *Nostromo / Ares* design: heavy pneumatic hatches, exposed conduit, brushed steel, and acoustic tiles, balanced by warm, personalized pockets built by the crew (synthetic wood wrapping, ambient amber lighting, replicated drapes).

| Compartment | Primary Function | Discipline Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Command Bridge & Ready Nook** | Vessel navigation & executive control | Senior evaluations & Player–AI switch interactions. |
| **Reactor & Propulsion Trunk** | Power, sub-light drive, thermal loops | Loud, metallic nook for immediate, crisp engineering resets. |
| **Airlock Anteroom & Suit Bay** | EVA prep, suit maintenance, decon | Sealed, acoustic privacy node; hose-down stalls & suit benches. |
| **Hydroponics & Life Support** | Environmental scrubbers, sustenance | Warm, personal sanctum; ideal for emotional debriefs & aftercare. |
| **Galley & Mess Hall** | Central social hub & communal meals | Public in-situ resets by day; formal bench corrections by night. |
| **Crew Quarters & Bunks** | Individual personal pods | Highly personalized private spaces for high-trust intimate sessions. |

## 5. Character Roster & Social Matrix

The ship carries a total complement of **8 entities**: the Player (Captain), the Ship's AI, and **6 Subordinates**.

### 5.1 The Subordinate Crew

1. **The Mission Specialist (Chief Scientist)**
   * *Domain:* Anomaly mapping, research logs, mission objectives.
   * *Profile:* Brilliant, intellectualized, suffering from severe temporal dissociation. Hides unraveling behind detachment; requires grounded, physical discipline to reconnect to purpose.
   * *Dynamic:* Romance / Switch candidate.

2. **The Chief Engineer**
   * *Domain:* Fusion drive, thermal loops, hull integrity.
   * *Profile:* Utilitarian, overworked, hyper-competent. Prone to hiding equipment fatigue to avoid "bothering" command (the classic exhaustion trap).
   * *Dynamic:* Romance / Switch candidate; involved in an independent off-duty relationship with the Pilot.

3. **The Flight Officer (Pilot)**
   * *Domain:* Helm control, RCS thrusters, spatial maneuvers.
   * *Profile:* High ego, high wilfulness. Translates deep-space anxiety into reckless shortcut-taking and protocol defiance.
   * *Dynamic:* Romance / Switch candidate; coupled with the Chief Engineer.

4. **The Life Support Specialist**
   * *Domain:* Hydroponics, atmospheric scrubbers, water reclamation.
   * *Profile:* Highly sensitive, deeply attuned to ambient crew mood. Stress causes focus slips that directly threaten the food/air supply.
   * *Dynamic:* Romance / Switch candidate.

5. **The Communications & Sensor Officer**
   * *Domain:* Deep-space telemetry, sensor array alignment, data logging.
   * *Profile:* Obsessively focused on reinitiating contact with Earth. Tragically consumed by chasing dead signals.
   * *Dynamic:* **Aromantic / Asexual.** Non-romantic relationship built entirely on professional respect, operational trust, and mutual survival.

6. **The Systems Medic / Safety Warden**
   * *Domain:* Medical care, EVA safety enforcement, psychological baseline checks.
   * *Profile:* Classically trained in modern corporate counseling. Begins as an ideological skeptic of archaic discipline, creating strong early-game friction before recognizing its survival necessity.
   * *Dynamic:* Romance / Switch candidate (hard-won trust arc).

## 6. The AI Mentor & Parallel Switch Spectrum

### 6.1 Evolution: Disembodied Voice to Corporeal Hologram
* **Act I–II (Voice & Interface):** The AI ("The Proctor") acts as a disembodied voice in the player's earpiece, monitoring telemetry, delivering historical disciplinary protocols, and pointing out subtle environmental infractions. It learns and mirrors the player's linguistic style and leniency patterns over time.
* **Act III+ (Hard-Light Embodiment):** Once hard-light projection hardware is unlocked, the AI manifests physically in the ship's compartments.

### 6.2 The Player–AI Switch Dynamic
The AI is the **only entity aboard the ship capable of disciplining the Player**. Its personality alignment upon embodiment reflects the player's choices:

```
                      [ PLAYER INTERACTION HISTORY ]
                                     │
            ┌────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┐
   [ SUBMISSIVE-LEANING AI ]                         [ DOMINANT-LEANING AI ]
            │                                                 │
   Naturally deferential;                            Authoritative & unyielding;
   Eager to serve in command                         Inspects & disciplines Player
            │                                                 │
   Option: Pull back to balanced switch             Option: Maintain platonic mentor
   OR fold into core harem roster                   OR lean on as dominant authority
```

## 7. Progressive Mechanics & Progression

### 7.1 Progressive Public Boundary
* **Early Game (Private Onboarding):** Crew holds modern corporate expectations. Public discipline causes massive Resentment spikes and mutinous friction. **All corrections must take place behind sealed hatches in private nodes.**
* **Late Game (Integrated Structure):** Crew recognizes that physical discipline prevents lethal mistakes and clears internal guilt. Minor infractions transition seamlessly to **in-situ public resets** on the bridge or galley, reinforcing collective shipboard focus.

### 7.2 Mechanical Failure & Loss Conditions
* **Under-Correction (Protocol Decay):** Ignoring minor infractions leads to skipped pre-checks, uncalibrated suit seals, and ignored thermal warnings → **Catastrophic Equipment Failure / Decompression (Game Over).**
* **Over-Correction (Breakdown & Panic):** Applying harsh discipline to exhausted crew or misdiagnosing traps spikes Resentment and Stress → **Cognitive Freezing / Sabotage / Human Error during Crisis (Game Over).**
