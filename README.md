# Project Meridian (working title)

A deep-space sci-fi psychological action-adventure and management sim, built on the discipline and pain engine from [Little Red’s Lessons](https://github.com/asonichero/redlessons), with the safe-word model of [Birchwood House](https://github.com/asonichero/fairy-shoe). Plans and open questions are in `docs/alpha-plan.md`.

## Run it

```
npm run serve        # http://localhost:8123/ opens the correction test (session.html); /figures.html shows one figure at a time (any crew member or the Captain, posed); /model.html the bodies and clothes
npm test             # the pain model, the scripted correction, the crew and their lines, the figures' rig (built in code and from the models)
```

The figures' bodies and clothes are built in Blender (4.0, headless) from a reference turnaround, and saved as `models/meridian_f.glb` and `models/meridian_m.glb` (with `models/meridian_meta.json`). To rebuild them:

```
python3 tools/measure-reference.py path/to/turnaround.jpg     # → tools/blender/reference.json (already measured, kept in the repo)
blender -b --python tools/blender/build_figures.py -- f m       # → models/meridian_{f,m}.glb, .blend, and meridian_meta.json
```

`model.html` shows them as a turnaround sheet: `?sex=f|m`, `?wear=uniform|offduty|down|under` (or a list of garments), `?key=muscle:1`, `?wire=1`, `?bones=1`, and `?overlay=<image>` to lay the reference over them.

## The correction test (`session.html`)

This is the first playable piece. It tests the control scheme, the models and the style. The setting is the Airlock Anteroom and Suit Bay, a private node.

- **Setup:** choose the position (across the Captain's knee on the suit bench, or at the wall gripping the rail with feet apart), and who is corrected, in order. Choose who witnesses, and where each crew member waits between turns (the corner, hands on head, as redlessons does; or standing aside with the witnesses).
  - Each crew member's gender is a toggle. Their baseline body type is translated to male or female.
  - The outfit is uniform or off duty. Off duty, the Captain keeps the henley (sleeves pushed up), canvas trousers with a contrast seam, and boots; each crew member wears their own (`Crew.ROSTER[id].offduty`), their style but practical at home aboard (they never make landfall: soft indoor shoes over socks, never boots):
    - **Lindqvist:** the simplest: base layers, comfort first, in soft purples and lilacs: a heathered lilac thermal (mock neck, ribbed cuffs and hem), loose deep plum joggers with ribbed ankle cuffs and a drawstring, lilac socks, soft felt shoes; a violet thread at the left cuff.
    - **Varga:** expressive, short of quirky: a bold botanical print (broad pale leaves on deep teal), the shirt worn open over a plain long-sleeved tee, olive chinos with slant and back pockets, navy canvas deck shoes.
    - **Okafor:** a dark henley, sleeves pushed up, under a worn leather waistcoat with brass buttons; heavy work trousers with double knees, a rule pocket and rust stitching; soft-soled leather work shoes.
    - **Ashdown:** a sunny yellow tee printed with small flowers, sleeves pushed up; faded red carpenter trousers with bellows pockets and a hammer loop; white trainers over yellow socks.
    - **Haddad:** a high-collared cream blouse under a deep green knitted sweater vest (down to the blouse's hem, ribbed at the V, the armholes and the hem), a soft scarf tied at the collar, pressed tapered trousers, neat flats.
    - **Ruel:** a crisp collared shirt under a mustard cable-knit cardigan, pressed grey slacks, polished loafers.
    The tops are the uniform's long-sleeved shape or the henley's pushed-up one; everything else (the layers, prints, pockets, collars and shoes) is drawn on them in the shaders, from each point's rest position, so it stays crisp and goes with the cloth.
  - Basic customisation for the Captain: gender, skin, hair colour and style, and build.
- **Controls:**
  - Runs of smacks start and stop on a toggle (**Space**), not in counted runs.
  - **Pace** (`[` `]`) and **strength** (`-` `=`), in steps of a quarter.
  - **Side**: alternate, left or right (**D**). **Where**: cheek, sit spot (the default) or thighs (**A**), placed from each body's own seat (measured in Blender): the cheek where it's fullest, the sit spot just above the fold beneath it, the thighs below the fold. Nothing lands above the seat.
  - **Soothe** (**S**): between runs, the palm in slow circles over every place a smack can land, each cheek, sit spot and thigh in turn, gliding from one to the next. It takes the sting down.
  - **Clothes** (**T B U**): each garment is its own mesh with a raised or lowered variant (top raised, trousers lowered to the knees, briefs lowered to mid-thigh). The Captain does it, and it's seen: the right hand goes to the garment's edge, grips it and carries it along them (the waistband down over the seat and the back of the thighs to the knees, or to mid-thigh for the briefs; the top's hem pushed up their back), and back the other way to restore it; the garment changes over midway, so the hand ends where the cloth now is. Several queue up in a sensible order. A change stops a run, and a new one can't start until it's done.
  - **End turn** (**Enter**): the Captain soothes them a little, then dresses them (briefs, trousers, then the top, each seen), and then on: to the next crew member's turn (they go to the corner or aside), or, if they were the last, a cut to the aftercare. They stay exactly where they are, both of them, until the cut. A **safe word** cuts straight to their aftercare, and the session goes on with anyone left after it.
  - **Cameras** (**C**, or the buttons).
- **Distress** is the pain they hold against what they can take, up to the edge of resistance. Past it, it follows the pain's logarithm, slowly at first and then faster, so the stretch from the edge to too harsh takes far more than the way to it (too harsh at 2 takes about 3.4 times the edge's pain): they cry steadily from 1.7 (eyes closed, tears streaking) and still at 1.8. Firm and steady (strength 1.5, pace 1.5, from bare skin) reaches the edge in about a minute and 1.4 two minutes after, levelling off around 1.6; at pace 2: the edge in half a minute, steady crying at about 1:50, still at about 2:10, too harsh and the safe word at about 3:00.
- **Consent:** each crew member has agreed a safe word (it's shown on screen). The player is the disciplinarian, so the trigger is the pain model's *too harsh* threshold. That bar is higher than redlessons' (2 against 1.5) and has to be held for 1.2 s, or reach 2.05, so corrections don't trend short. When it's crossed, the crew member calls their word and everything stops.
- **The body's response** (purely visual for now): a correction sends blood to the sex, in men and women alike. It builds slowly over a correction (blood takes its time: a third of the way in a minute, near its height after two or three), with each smack (less through cloth), the warmth of the marks and soothing, and fades over a couple of minutes once it's over (through aftercare). His is fuller and longer and lifts, straight, on its own bone, eased up slowly; with no response it hangs with gravity whichever way the body lies, and it hangs clear of the scrotum, so lifting it comes away clean. The scrotum swings with gravity on a bone of its own (never with the lift). Across the knee both are tucked well forward, out of harm's way: the shaft lies along the Captain's lap, and the scrotum is drawn up into the crotch and swung forward in front of it, so from behind nothing hangs between the thighs where a stray stroke could land. Hers, the folds fuller along their whole length (they stay folds, never rounder at one place), parting a little, the inner form fuller; in both a deeper, rosier tone there. In these corrections it goes partway at most (half the range); later ones where desire is present can let it go all the way (`opts.desire`). Each crew member has their own scale (`body.response`, 1 for everyone for now): it's a bodily response, not a measure of desire, so the asexual crew member is no different. The Captain is affected too, emotionally as well as physically: his builds more slowly, from giving the correction, and he keeps most of it in check (about a third shows), less so as the affection between him and the one in his care grows and is returned (`dynamic.bond`, not yet tracked, 0 for now); it shows in his trousers' pouch filling out, and a little colour in his face. The figures viewer has a slider for it.
- **Scripted responses** per character: as they're placed, at the first smack, as their distress reaches each band, now and then while it's hard, when soothed, the safe word, and at the end.
- **Sound:** levels for master, effects, voices, music and ambience, in the set-up (☰); kept between visits.
- **Graphics:** *Full* or *Low-CPU*, in the set-up (☰), switchable mid-session and kept between visits (`?low=1` / `?low=0` to force it). Low-CPU keeps the figures (models, skinning, outlines, shape keys), their shadows and all the animation exactly as they are, and gives way in the room: the room's own meshes stop casting shadows (only the figures cast, from the pendant, on a smaller map) and the wall light's shadow goes; the three small coloured lamps go (the sky fill a little up to make up); small details (hatch bolts and spokes, conduit brackets, rail fixings, the plant's leaves, the mug) aren't drawn; each wall is drawn as a few merged meshes rather than a panel at a time; no bloom, no multisampling, one pixel per pixel. In the suit bay that's about 45% fewer draw calls a frame.
- **The HUD** sits down the right of the screen: the crew member's name and agreed word, the cameras, and the controls (run, soothe, end turn; pace, strength; side, where; clothes; the meter). The dialogue is at the bottom, centred. On a desktop each camera keeps what it looks at in the centre of the screen.
- **On a phone:** held upright, the controls take the lower third of the screen, laid out in rows, with the dialogue just above them and the cameras in a row under the crew member's name and agreed word; on its side, the controls are a sidebar down the right, the cameras down the left and the dialogue along the bottom between. The set-up opens as a sheet over the view (☰), touch targets are larger, and the view widens when upright and is centred in what the controls leave visible.
- **After:** a conversation with each crew member in turn, seated side by side on the bench. Aftercare is delivered through what's said (reassure, say why it mattered, or ask how they are), followed by an instruction for the rest of the shift (rest detail, log reset, eat together, or back to station). Its lines are click-through: each waits for a click on the dialogue box (or Space or Enter; a click while it's typing finishes it), except the line a choice hangs on, which stays up with the choices under it.

## The look

The style is redlessons' toon look on bodies sculpted after a stylised reference turnaround:
- **The bodies** (`tools/blender/build_figures.py`): the female body is measured from the reference's front and side silhouettes, row by row: the trunk, legs and arms each a surface of measured rows (the bust and seat as forms on the chest and the lower back, the thighs taking over from the hips across the crotch), the hands and feet modelled, all smoothly united and drawn by marching cubes. Its proportions are made realistic where the reference is stylised: the crotch is lowered 5 cm (the reference's long legs shortened, the trunk lengthened to match, head and shoulders kept), putting it at about 47% of the height. The male is derived from it: broader shoulders and chest, narrower hips, a flat chest, a fuller neck.
- **The rig:** the game's own skeleton (below), placed on each body and weighted automatically. Shape keys move each body toward the extremes of build (muscle, slight, soft, firm) and, for her, the bust toward smaller or much larger (the larger also fuller, lower and wider, not only further out); each crew member's baseline build and bust size set them. The seats are a little fuller than the reference's, the men's too, within realistic body types. The seat's curve is rounded up and down (rising gently from the small of the back, no flat face behind), and where it meets the thighs the two forms' blend is evened out over a few centimetres of height, so it rounds under into the fold without a dent. A fuller build closes the cleft between the cheeks (and the gap between the thighs) but never quite shuts it, so the two sides never pass through each other. Her sex is modelled in as much detail as his: two outer folds, each rising out of the front of the torso at the mons (blending into it), running down round the front of the crotch, fullest low at the front, hanging a little below it between the thighs (meeting them at a crease, the inner thighs eased back slightly at their tops so a small gap opens below them), and on back toward the perineum; the crease between them where they meet, and in it, set back, the inner form, a deeper rose, seen as they part. They go with the pelvis (so they stay together as the hips roll and the legs part), only the folds' lower outer edges sharing a little with the thighs, so they ease open as the legs spread. Nothing painted but the inner form's tone; otherwise the forms' own shading and a finer outline over them. They don't change with the build keys, and the thighs never fill in over them; each goes with its own thigh (mostly the thigh at the bottom, mostly the pelvis where they meet the mons), so they stay with the thighs as the hips roll; the briefs' gusset is carried down smoothly over them; his sex is a simple scrotum and penis, hanging, rooted well up in the body so it joins without a crease, with shape keys toward smaller or larger (girth and length) set by the same number as the crew member's bust size in the female translation; the male Captain's is the largest. The outlines are drawn a touch behind the surfaces, and stand out less where the skin faces itself close across a crease (the cleft, between the thighs, under the bust: measured in the build with each shape key, as a per-point weight), so the two sides' outlines never reach through each other. The neck's upper part follows the head as well as the neck, and the nape, from just above the collar up under the skull, is left to the head's own skin, so as the head tips back (bent at a rail, looking forward) the back of the neck never shows through the back of the head or opens a gap above the collar.
- **The clothes** are layers drawn as shells over the body: each row's outline made convex (cloth bridges the hollows) and hanging from the bust, shoulder blades and seat rather than following the body in, cut clean at the hem, collar, cuffs, waist and leg openings, and weighted from the skin under it with the body's shape keys. Each layer is kept clear of the one under it. A top's body takes its weights from the trunk only and its sleeves from the arms, so none of it follows the arms when they're raised. The uniform's jacket (stand collar, long sleeves) and the off-duty shirt (open neck, sleeves rolled), each down or raised and rolled above the waist; trousers, up or lowered and bunched round the knees; briefs (trunks for the men), up or lowered round the thighs. Lowered, each is still one piece: the waistband stretched across the legs front and back, and the seat, crotch and front slung between them as a trough with the gusset sagging at its bottom. His sex is held in a pouch, a rounded form close against him rather than its own shape, sized by his shape keys; under the briefs or trousers, all of it is hidden, however far it hangs. Boots.
- The head, face and hair are still built in code (below), on the head bone. Figures fall back to the bodies built in code if the models don't load.

For every figure:
- **Skeleton:** real adult proportions, with the head a little larger for the toon lean (`HEAD_SCALE` in `js/figure.js`; 1 is realistic, redlessons used 1.34). The rig has:
  - a pelvis, two spine joints and clavicles;
  - hands with four two-jointed fingers and a two-jointed thumb, set with a grip (curl, spread, thumb);
  - a bone in each buttock for the flesh's give.
- **Skinning:** dual-quaternion, applied to the body, the clothes, the outlines and the shadows. Elbows, knees, shoulders and hips keep their volume as they bend instead of collapsing or pinching.
- **Surface** (the figures built in code): the trunk, limbs and neck are one continuous surface. Each profile is interpolated on a smooth curve between its rows, so silhouettes flow rather than facet. Muscle forms are sculpted into the profiles and softened under clothing: pecs, shoulder blades and the spine's groove; deltoid, biceps, triceps and forearm; quads, the inner quad above the knee, the kneecap, the calves and the ankle bones.
- **Shading:** soft three-band toon shading, ink outlines (an inverted hull) and a faint rim light. Every open edge of a garment (a waistband, leg opening, hem, cuff or collar) also has its own slim line of ink, so it reads as drawn from any side and covers where the cloth meets the skin. Characters cast shadows but don't receive them, which keeps the shading clean, as in redlessons. Garment details (the closure edge, belt and buckle, collar, hem, the shoulder flash and cuff bands, knee panels, seams and buttons) are drawn per pixel from rest-pose coordinates, so they stay crisp at any distance.
- **Faces:** redlessons' head shape and painted face layer (eyes, lids, brows, mouth, blush, tears), redrawn as expressions ease.
- **Lighting:** the room is moody, with cool steel, amber pockets and bloom on the lights.

## Poses and animation

- **Across the knee:** the Captain sits at the bench's end turned 45° to their right, so the lap points off its corner and nothing of the crew member's is over the bench. The crew member lies with the pelvis over the Captain's right thigh and the belly over the left, the back rounding down past it, the hands braced on the deck (or hanging, fingertips to it, where they don't reach), the legs down to the deck on the other side, the head turned to the room. Where they lie comes from where the Captain's lap actually is. Their back lies level across both thighs and bends down only past the left one, so nothing sinks into the Captain's knee. The Captain sits fairly upright (room for the swing), the left forearm resting along the small of their back with the hand curving over the far hip, the right hand resting on the seat between smacks.
- **At the wall:** bent well forward from the hips with a long back, each hand gripping the rail on its own side, over the top (fingers wrapped round it), feet apart. The Captain stands right beside them at their left, facing their side, hips level with theirs, feet planted wide either side of their nearest foot, knees a little bent and leaning in, the left arm resting along their shoulders, the hand on the far one.
- **The strike:** the forearm swings up from an elbow that stays low and a little back at the Captain's side, the hand rising to about shoulder height with the wrist cocked back, the torso turning a little into it. It comes down with the wrist held back until the last moment, snaps flat onto the skin, gives a little coming off, and settles.
- **Reactions** are springs, kicked by each smack and driven by how much they're fighting it:
  - **Each smack:** across the knee, the leg kicks on the side struck, the hips press down into the lap, the back arches, the head comes up, and the hands push and grip; at the wall, they come up onto their toes and the hips drive toward the bulkhead. The struck cheek gives and springs back.
  - **Agitation** rises with distress, from nothing while they're composed, through flinching and struggling, to its height at the edge of resistance. The crew know it's for their good and try to hold their position: what moves, until they're still, is involuntary. **The fight in them** goes out of them well past the edge (from 1.4), and by 1.8, just short of too harsh, they've stilled: the player's tell to wind down. Still, they answer each smack, only less (a smaller kick and flinch, crying steadily, the voice smaller and not every time), breathing hard, head hanging, hands loosening; and they say less, and less in words (a breath, a fragment, now and then). As distress falls the fight slowly returns.
  - **Temperament:** some take it more stoically than others. The smaller they are, the more they wriggle (their height in that translation and their mass), a little less the more pain they can take (`Crew.wriggle`; `body.wriggle` overrides it): Haddad most, then Ashdown, Varga and Lindqvist, Ruel, and Okafor, the most stoic. It scales how much of their agitation shows, and how big each smack's reaction is.
  - **Across the knee:** kicks come from the knee, the lower leg flicking up and the foot leaving the deck (on a smack, and now and then as they squirm). Squirming: the hips rolling and pulling away from the side struck, the back twisting, the legs scissoring, each on their own irregular rhythm. At its worst an occasional buck (the hips heaving up off the lap), and stepping in bouts. The hands won't stay put: one comes up off the deck a moment, or starts back toward the seat as if to cover it, and they stop it halfway.
  - **Faces:** composed, their own; then wincing, then gritted teeth; past the edge, tears welling, the eyes screwed shut as each smack lands; from 1.7, crying steadily.
  - **At the wall:** no wriggling. They step, in bouts of a few seconds (a foot coming off the deck, then the other), now and then once it really hurts, and stamp now and then on a hard smack; neither starts straight away or goes on throughout. The shoulders hunch on a smack, and now and then a hand lets go of the rail and grips it again.
  - **The men** don't kick from the hip or stamp: at the rail their stepping is a shift of the weight, a heel coming up, the foot staying on the deck.
- **Everyone else** stands planted in contrapposto: witnesses with arms folded, at parade rest or hands clasped; whoever's waiting their turn with hands clasped, head down; those done in the corner, feet together, hands on head.
- **Aftercare:** side by side on the bench, the crew member turned a little to the Captain, hands in their lap; the Captain's arm round them, the hand resting on their far shoulder (the elbow low behind their back), his other hand on his own knee. He looks at them, head turned and tipped to their face, wearing care: warmth and authority in balance (steady eyes, the brow a touch low and level, a kind mouth), softer where there's desire between them. The closer they've come (below), the closer they sit, hip to hip and leg to leg at the most: measured from the two bodies (each one's own thigh and calf), so they touch without going through each other and the broader get more room. With submission and desire they lean their head onto his shoulder, their elbows in at their sides; his body angles to take them, leaning a touch away so his head stays clear of theirs (or just touches), his face comes round almost to the front, only a little toward them, his eyes on them, and his hand moves to between their shoulder blades and rubs there in slow circles (the elbow dropped by his side, the forearm up their back). With desire and comfort both high, their near leg goes over his, the knee resting on his thigh, the shin hanging inside it, and his other hand rests on that knee. If their defiance is still standing at the end, or they're resentful, they hold him off: further along the bench, not leaning in, arms folded (still defiant), the head turned aside and up, short with him (a guarded opening line); he gives them the room, both hands on his own knees, his face keeping more of its authority.
- **The relationship:** each crew member's submission, desire and comfort with the Captain and the method, 0–1, will move through the story; for now they're sliders in the set-up (☰), per crew member, kept between visits and live in a session, with the defiance they bring and the resentment they carry (below). All three bring them closer in the aftercare. Lindqvist, asexual, has no desire to speak of: it stays at nothing for them. In a correction:
  - **Submission:** they hold their position better (hands leave the deck less, hardly reach back, buck less), still sooner, keep the head bowed rather than turned to the room, plead less and accept more ("Yes, Captain."), and at the end stay down until they're told (the less submissive start to come up as soon as it's over).
  - **Comfort:** it lowers their inhibitions: they wriggle more and are more vocal, dread the waiting hand less (so each smack hurts a little less), soothing works faster, the fight comes back sooner between runs, and their grip on the rail is looser.
  - **Desire:** their body's response comes faster and goes further (all the way at the top), the flush spreads to the face and up the chest, soothing feeds it more (and a shier face), the breath quickens, and once it shows they say something embarrassed. The Captain guards his own less as it's returned.
- **Defiance** (the GDD's Wilfulness and Resentment): what they bring to a correction, 0–1: their wilfulness (`meters.wilfulness`: Ashdown 0.75, Ruel 0.6, Lindqvist 0.35, Varga 0.3, Okafor 0.15, Haddad 0.1) unless set, plus half of any resentment they carry. It's attitude, never a withdrawal of consent: it moves nothing about the safe word, the edge, or the stillness tell. It drains as the correction lands: it can fall no further than how much it hurts lets it (gone by about the edge; sooner the more submissive, later the more there was), each smack taking a share of what's left. Drained while it's proportionate it becomes **Compliance**; drained by going well past the edge, or by main force (smacks at full strength), it becomes **Resentment**, as do smacks run on long after they've stilled, and a safe word. Resentment carries over (the set-up's slider) until aftercare clears half of it. The HUD shows defiance as it drains, and the summary what it became. While it lasts:
  - **As they're placed** they're slow to settle: up off the knee, or upright at the rail, looking back, till he puts them down ("Down.").
  - **Pride:** below the edge they hold back what they show (less squirming, fewer cries), so it breaks bigger: a sob, the head dropping, tears, and a line as it goes ("Okay— okay, you win, you win—").
  - **Head up**, and now and then a look back over the shoulder at the Captain; at the rail they stand taller to it (he straightens to give them room). Their face set against it, the teeth gritted once it's hard; soothed, sulking (and soothing reaches them less).
  - **On purpose:** coming up out of position (pushing up off the knee, straightening off the rail) till he puts them back; and across the knee, the far hand back over the seat, flat on it. He tells them to move it the first time; the second, he takes the wrist and holds it at the small of their back for the rest of it. The run waits while they're out of position.
  - **Back-talk** in place of the early lines and the pleas, in their own voice (Ashdown: "It was a good line and you know it!"; Ruel: "There's no evidence base for this, Captain."; Lindqvist: "Objection logged.").
- **Rooms** (docs/ship.md): pick the room in the set-up, then the positions it offers. **The suit bay**: across the knee on the suit bench, standing at the grab rail. **The galley and mess** (js/mess.js; starboard amidships, open along the spine corridor, the suit bay's and med bay's doors across it): an open galley along the hull with an island, the long crew table with a bench each side, a sofa, an armchair and a floor lamp aft, the crew's shelves and the clocks for ship's time and Earth's; across the knee on the end of the spine-side bench, standing with palms flat on the island, **over the table** (chest down over the fore end of the crew table, hands holding its sides, feet on the deck; the hips driven into the edge on a smack, up onto the toes, a lower leg kicking up now and then), aftercare on the sofa. **Crew quarters** (js/quarters.js; one cabin as issued, the same for all six before anyone makes it their own: 3 m along the ship by 6 m from the hull to the spine; down the fore wall from the hull, a night table as wide as the bunk with a lamp on it, the bunk (its head at the night table, drawers under it, a padded headboard and a shelf over it, a curtain on a track drawn back to its head), built-in storage from the bunk's foot to the head, and the head in its own cubicle by the way in, its door opening aft onto the entrance; along the hull from the night table, a cork board, a work surface at desk height to sit or stand at (with a stool), the view panel over it and a plant at its end; on the aft wall toward the hull, a fold-down desk with a stool and the cabin's terminal, the rest of that wall clear; a rug in front of the bunk). Built for decades away, it's made for the people in it rather than issued like a cell: the ship's steel bones show (the ribs, the ceiling beam, the door's frame), but everything a person lives against is warm, as in the mess (painted panels over a wood dado, pale oak furniture, cream storage, fabric, carpet, an acoustic ceiling); light kept to the ship's day (an even ceiling panel by day, warm amber coves and the lamp at night); privacy at the bunk; something growing; somewhere to pin things; and a **view panel**: there's nothing outside worth looking at for long at their speed, so every cabin has a window onto somewhere calm (a still lake under hills, under the stars at night). It's the subject's own cabin, and **private**: nobody watches, so witnesses, and anyone waiting their turn, stay outside. Across the knee with the Captain perched on the edge of the bunk, square to it (they lie along it in front, the head toward the pillow end, the legs toward the foot); standing with palms flat on the aft wall between the desk and the way in, bent well forward (far enough along that the Captain, at their left, stands clear of the desk); aftercare side by side on the edge of the bunk. A **time of day** in the set-up: by day the mess is lit warm and even by LED strips along its rafters, a straw light against the corridor's harsh white; at night the strips go down to a glow and it's the amber pendants over the table and the lamps; in a cabin, the ceiling panel by day, the coves and the night table's lamp at night. Each room gives its seats, what's held when standing (a rail, a counter's edge, a wall), its table, its aftercare seat, where witnesses stand and its cameras; the correction poses in those frames.
- **Nothing through the deck:** after each pose, the tip of each foot and the fingertips are checked against the deck, and a leg or arm that would go under it is drawn up and posed again (up on the toes at the rail, the toes pointed across the knee, the hands on the deck).
- **Fades:** a short fade covers each change of set-up (a new turn, a change of position, the aftercare), so nobody snaps into place.
- **Nothing in the way:** whatever comes between the camera and the two in the middle (a fitting of the bay, someone watching) is hidden while it's there; it still casts its shadow. When the camera comes through a wall (or into what's on it), that whole side goes: the wall, its ribs, pipes, lockers and alcoves (each fitting knows which wall, or the ceiling, it's on: `bay.sideOf`).
