Relationship

Where each crew member is with you and the method, as the story goes (set here for now). Desire speeds their body's response and lets it go further; comfort lowers their inhibitions, so they wriggle more; all three bring them closer in the aftercare, and submission and desire have them lean into you. Defiance is what they bring to the correction (their wilfulness to start with): it drains as it lands, into Compliance if you keep it proportionate, into Resentment if you go too far. Resentment carries over (half of it adds to their defiance next time) until aftercare clears it.
